Full-stack · Learning tool

Spaced-Repetition Recall Tracker

A flashcard app that shows each card again at the right time, so you remember it longer. I use it every day for DSA revision.

  • After each card, you rate how well you remembered it. Hard cards come back sooner and easy cards later.
  • A progress dashboard shows how much you retain, how each category is going, and your daily review streak.
  • Secure login, and you can add, edit and delete your own cards and categories.
